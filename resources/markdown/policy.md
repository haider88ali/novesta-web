# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: :updated*

This policy explains what information :app collects, why, where it goes and the choices you have. :app is run by :operator ("we", "us"). Contact us at **:email** about anything in this policy.

**In short:** your conversations, memories, reminders and files stay on your computer. Requests you send to the AI pass through our servers to our AI provider and back, but we don't store their content. We keep your account details, billing status and usage counts. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI.

## 1. What stays on your computer

The :app desktop app stores these only on your device:

- your conversation history, memories ("remember that…"), reminders and routines;
- your settings (language, voice, personality) and the project folder you opened;
- designs, generated code, meeting notes, downloads and screenshots it saves for you;
- your device sign-in token, encrypted with your system keychain.

You can delete them in the app (history, memories, reminders) or by removing :app's data folder. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete our server copy of your account; see section 8.

## 2. What we collect on our servers

- **Account:** your name, email address, hashed password and, if you sign in with GitHub or Google, your ID with that provider.
- **Devices:** a name for each signed-in device and when it was last used, so you can see and sign them out.
- **Usage records:** for each AI request, the type (message, design, voice, transcription), model, token counts, number of web searches, voice minutes, estimated cost, time and an AI response reference. We use these to enforce your plan's limits, show your usage and prevent abuse. **They don't include what you said or what :app answered.**
- **Billing:** your plan, subscription status and Paddle customer and transaction IDs. Card and payment details are handled by Paddle; we never see your full card number.
- **Technical logs:** IP address, browser and error details in our server logs, kept for security and fixing problems.

## 3. What is sent to our AI provider

When you ask :app something, the app sends the request through our server to **OpenAI**, which runs the AI models. Depending on what you ask, this can include:

- your message, and your recorded voice to turn into text;
- text :app reads aloud to you with an AI voice;
- context :app needs to answer well: the date and time, your saved memories and routines, and earlier messages in the conversation;
- the contents of files or code :app reads for you, results of commands you approved, and a screenshot of your screen only when you ask :app to look at it;
- your descriptions for UI designs.

Web searches are performed by OpenAI's search tool. OpenAI processes this data under its API business terms. It does **not** use API data to train its models, and it keeps it for a limited time (generally up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring and so :app can continue a conversation. Our server passes requests through and doesn't save their content.

## 4. Other services

- **Paddle** processes payments, taxes and invoices as our Merchant of Record and has its own privacy policy.
- **Prayer times:** when you ask for them, the app sends your city (not your exact location) to the Aladhan prayer-times service.
- **WhatsApp:** :app can look up a name in your computer's Contacts to find a number. This happens on your computer, and the message is only sent if you press send in WhatsApp.
- **Figma:** designs go from the app to the :app Figma plugin on your own computer.
- **Email:** we use an email provider to send sign-in, account and billing emails.
- **Hosting:** our website and database run on a hosting provider that stores the data in section 2.

We share data only with these providers, as needed to run :app, or when the law requires it. If :app is ever sold or merged, your data would move to the new owner under this policy, and we'd tell you first.

## 5. Why we use your data

- To provide :app and your plan (performance of our contract with you).
- To prevent fraud and abuse, keep :app secure and fix problems (our legitimate interests).
- To meet tax, accounting and legal duties (legal obligation).
- To send emails about your account, billing and important changes. We don't send marketing emails unless you opt in.

## 6. Cookies

The website uses only essential cookies: one to keep you signed in, one for security (CSRF protection) and one to remember your light/dark theme choice. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

## 7. How long we keep data

- Account and device data: while your account is open.
- Usage records: up to 24 months, for limits, billing questions and abuse prevention.
- Billing records: as long as tax law requires (usually up to 7 years), mostly held by Paddle.
- Server logs: up to 30 days.

## 8. Your rights

You can see and update your account details on your profile page, sign out devices on your dashboard, and **delete your account** from your profile at any time; this cancels any subscription and removes your account, devices and usage records from our servers, except billing records we must keep by law. You can also email **:email** to ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, or to object to how we use it. We'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, including complaining to your data-protection authority.

## 9. Security

Traffic to our website and AI service is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, device tokens can be revoked any time and are stored encrypted on your computer, and our AI keys never leave our server. The app only runs commands and changes files after you approve them, and it refuses to read secret files such as `.env` files and private keys. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at :email if you find a problem.

## 10. Children

:app isn't meant for children under 13 (or the higher age required where you live). We don't knowingly collect their data; contact us and we'll delete it.

## 11. International transfers

Our providers, including OpenAI and Paddle, may process data in countries other than yours, such as the United States. We rely on their contractual safeguards for these transfers.

## 12. Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and tell you by email or in the app about significant changes.
